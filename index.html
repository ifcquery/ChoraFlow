Choraflow

A WebAssembly viewer for Autodesk Revit® files (.rvt, .rfa, .rte, .rft). The file never leaves your computer.

Revit files are read by Choraflow's own clean-room reader, which draws each element from the 3-D representation Revit saved in the file.

Mouse: left drag orbits about the point under the cursor (or the last one picked), right or middle drag (or Shift + left) pans holding the point under the cursor, wheel zooms toward the surface under the cursor, click selects (Ctrl adds), double-click focuses the point under the cursor (on the background: fits the model). Keys: F fit, H hide, I isolate, A show all, X x-ray, Esc clear.
